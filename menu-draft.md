# Notes on the extraction

- **No item appears twice.** Beyond the in-sheet repeats below, *Crevettes grillées* on the
  Abou Mazloum poster was the same sandwich as *Sandwich crevettes grillées* on the Salinas
  sheet, so only one is kept. The grilled/crispy/creamy shrimp **plates** are separate dishes
  from the shrimp **sandwiches** and both are kept.
- **Duplicates in the source.** "Grillade de poisson signature" is printed twice on the
  grilled-fish sheet; the later layout shows the second slot is **Poisson doré croustillant**,
  so that is what it became here. **Moutabal** is also printed twice. Both deduplicated.
- **Two dishes both called "Poulpe."** Renamed from their own ingredients to
  *Poulpe aux herbes* and *Poulpe citronné*, so guests can tell them apart.
- **Two different restaurants.** The blue posters are Abou Mazloum; everything under Viande and
  Poulet comes from there, and those are sandwiches, not plates.
- **Fajita Philadelphie** is filed under Viande on the usual reading of Philly steak, but the
  source does not say — move it to Poulet if that is wrong.
- **Soupes is empty** because the photos have none. Nothing invented to fill it.
